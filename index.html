What is Aarvex

A verified marketplace built for every business

Aarvex Global is a B2B trading portal where any business — retail, wholesale, manufacturing, services or agri — can discover suppliers, negotiate prices, pay online or on delivery, and track every order. Sellers are KYC-verified, invoices are GST-ready, and the whole flow lives in one login. No middlemen, no guesswork — just professional trade.

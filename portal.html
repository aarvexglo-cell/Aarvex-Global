Privacy & Policy

Aarvex Global collects your name, email, phone, and order details to pre-fill forms and process trade requests. KYC documents are encrypted in AWS S3 and accessed only by authorised admins for verification.

How we use your data

Profile data auto-fills order forms. KYC data verifies seller identity. We never share seller contact details with buyers — all deals are mediated by Aarvex Global.

KYC security

Aadhaar numbers are masked (last 4 digits only stored). Document images use presigned URLs and are never publicly accessible.

Your rights

You may request account deletion via support. See our full policy at privacy-policy.html.

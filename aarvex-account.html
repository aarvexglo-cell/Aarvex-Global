Privacy & Policy

Last updated · 1 August 2026

Aarvex Global aapki privacy ko seriously leta hai. Ye policy batati hai ki hum kaunsa data collect karte hain aur usse kaise use karte hain.

1. Data we collect

Account banate waqt aur platform use karte waqt hum ye information collect karte hain:

  • Naam, email, phone number aur city
  • KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN, bank details) — sirf verification ke liye
  • Orders, listings, chats aur delivery location
  • Device & usage data (app improve karne ke liye)

2. How we use it

Aapka data sirf platform chalane ke liye use hota hai:

  • Buyers aur sellers ko connect karna
  • Orders, payments aur delivery process karna
  • Fraud rokna aur trust score banana
  • Zaroori updates aur alerts bhejna

Hum aapka personal data kisi third party ko bechte nahi hain.

3. KYC & payments

KYC documents encrypted store hote hain aur sirf verification team dekhti hai. Bank details sirf payouts ke liye use hoti hain aur payment partner ke through securely process hoti hain. Aap verification ke baad documents delete karne ki request kar sakte hain.

4. Your rights

Aapke paas ye rights hain:

  • Apna data dekhna aur download karna
  • Galat information correct karana
  • Account aur data delete karana
  • Marketing messages se opt-out karna

5. Contact

Privacy se related koi sawaal ho to hume privacy@aarvexglobal.com pe email karein ya settings se support chat kholein.
